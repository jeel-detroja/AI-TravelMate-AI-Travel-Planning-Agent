How it works

1. You describe the trip

Destination, dates, budget, travellers, interests, style and pace.

2. We gather verified data

The destination is geocoded, real attractions are fetched from OpenStreetMap, and the forecast comes from Open-Meteo.

3. A planner arranges the days

Gemini (if configured) or a deterministic rule-based planner organises places into morning, afternoon and evening. The AI may only choose from verified places; its output is validated by the server.

4. You get a clear, labelled plan

Costs are computed on the server from stated assumptions, never taken from the AI.
