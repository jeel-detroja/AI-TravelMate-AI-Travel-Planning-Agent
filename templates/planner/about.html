About AI TravelMate

AI TravelMate is an AI-powered travel planning assistant. Give it a destination, dates, budget and interests, and it produces a day-by-day itinerary with a map, weather forecast and a budget estimate.

Data sources

  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) — destination lookup.
  • Overpass API (OpenStreetMap) — real attractions near the destination.
  • Open-Meteo — daily weather forecast.
  • Google Gemini (optional) — arranges verified places into a plan; a rule-based planner is used otherwise.

Honest limitations

  • Costs are rough, assumption-based estimates — not live prices or bookings.
  • Opening hours, ticket prices, traffic and availability are not provided.
  • Weather is only available for about 16 days ahead.
  • Place coverage depends on how well OpenStreetMap maps the area.
