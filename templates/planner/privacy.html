Privacy

We store your account details and the trips you create so you can view them later. Trips are only visible to you.

To build an itinerary, your destination text is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim, approximate coordinates are sent to Overpass and Open-Meteo, and — only if the optional Gemini provider is enabled — your trip preferences and a list of public place names are sent to Google Gemini. No password or personal contact details are sent to these services.

You can delete any trip at any time from its page.
